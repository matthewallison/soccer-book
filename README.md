# Soccer Development Curriculum

A practical U13–U16 player-development and team-coaching framework built around intelligent, technical, courageous soccer.

The central goal is not to create players who memorize instructions. It is to develop players who can **scan, communicate, make intentional decisions, solve problems, compete, reflect, and adapt**.

## Core team mantras

### Practice
**SCAN. TALK. INTENTION. FASTER.**

### Games
**CALM MIND. FAST SOCCER.**

### After mistakes
**RESPOND. REFLECT. LEARN. NEXT PLAY.**

## Philosophy

Winning matters, and players should want to win. But the scoreboard is not the primary measure of development.

**Winning is not the primary measure of development, but competing to win is part of development.**

The system itself should also keep improving. Coaches should be attached to sound principles, not rigidly attached to one method. Training, roles, constraints, and tactical details should evolve as players develop and the team reveals new needs.
