# Soccer Development Handbook

A practical player-development and team-coaching handbook for a U13–U14 girls' team, built around intelligent, technical, courageous soccer. The framework applies across U13–U16.

The central goal is not to create players who memorize instructions. It is to develop players who can **scan, communicate, make intentional decisions, solve problems, compete, reflect, lead, and adapt**.

[Download the printable handbook (PDF)](soccer-development-handbook.pdf){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## Core team mantras

Players say the mantras out loud, together: the practice mantra is the first thing at every practice, and the game mantra is the first thing in every game warm-up.

| Moment | Mantra |
|---|---|
| Practice | **SCAN. TALK. INTENTION. FASTER.** |
| Games | **CALM MIND. FAST SOCCER.** |
| After mistakes | **RESPOND. REFLECT. LEARN. NEXT PLAY.** |
| Pressure moments | **WANT THE MOMENT.** |

## Where to start

### Players

- Know the four mantras by memory. Learn the bold principles in the [High-Level Player Sheet](player-philosophy.md) over time, and use it as a quick reference.
- Read [Team Culture, Courage, Leadership & Self-Coaching](team-culture-and-leadership.md) for the ideas behind the sheet.
- Before each match, review the [position card](positions/index.md) for your role and picture its moments.
- Study your games with the [Film Review Guide](film-review-guide.md), and choose short sessions from the [Home Development Menu](home-development.md).
- If something hurts, tell a parent and your coach, and read [Injuries, Recovery, and Return to Play](injuries-and-return-to-play.md).

### Position cards

- Find your role by number in the [position card index](positions/index.md): one page to read before every match.

### Coaches

- Teach from the [Coaching Game Model & Development Manual](coaching-game-model.md). Its "Where to start" list shows how to build the model in layers.
- Check the environment against [What Good Coaching Looks Like](good-coaching-signs.md).
- Plan and follow up with the [Weekend Role Sheet](templates/weekend-role-sheet.md), [Game Reflection](templates/game-reflection.md), and [Development Plan](templates/development-plan.md).

### Parents

- Start with [Parent Partnership](parent-partnership.md).
- Use [What Good Coaching Looks Like](good-coaching-signs.md) to see whether the team is learning, not just winning.

## The middle gear

Players should be able to combine in tight spaces, but also recognize when pressure has been escaped and the field has opened.

**SMALL SPACE: COMBINE. BIG SPACE: EXPLOIT.**

The full explanation and training progression are in §7 of the [Coaching Game Model](coaching-game-model.md).
