# Center Back (#4 / #5) — Position Card

## My purpose

Protect the center, lead the back line, and start our attacks with composure.

## Pregame mindset

**Calm is a weapon.**

**Safe does not automatically mean clearance.** Near your own goal, if you have time and space, solve the soccer problem — even after a pass goes wrong. Talk early: tell the line to step, drop, or cover. **CALM MIND. FAST SOCCER.**

## Picture these moments

- Picture receiving from the goalkeeper: you check your shoulder, open your body, and touch it forward.
- Picture nobody pressing you: you carry until a defender steps, then find the midfielder she left.
- Picture the ball side getting crowded: you switch it to the open side.
- Picture their press pushing high and a far-side winger free: you hit a purposeful diagonal into her path.
- Picture an attacker running at you on a counter: you stay on your feet, slow her down, and wait for help.
- Picture your partner stepping to win the ball: you tuck in behind her and cover the space she left.

## When we have it

- Your partner is an option, not an automatic pass.
- Carry sideways to change the angle when the forward lane is closed.
- Find midfield feet when the lane is real; after you pass, step up to support.

## When we lose it

- Protect the central route to goal first, then step, delay, or recover.
- If an outside back is caught high, delay the counter until she recovers.
- Recover on an angle toward goal, not by chasing.

## When they have it

- Ball pressured: hold the line or push up together.
- Ball unpressured: drop a few steps together — moving as one line manages offside.
- Crosses: stay goal-side, see ball and runner, attack the ball in front of you.
- Read the pass early; intercept when you can win it.

## When we win it

- Scan forward: if a real transition advantage exists, use it.
- If not, secure the ball and start possession calmly.

## Important relationships

**One steps, one covers.**

- The other center back (#4 / #5): don't both go to the same attacker, and don't both leave her free.
- Pivots (#6 / #8): they give you an angle and protect the space in front of you. Delay counters together.
- Goalkeeper (#1): your outlet and your eyes behind you. Keep her available; listen to her calls.
- Outside backs (#2 / #3): use them as early wide outlets, and keep them connected to your line.

## When you're unsure

**Protect the center. Stay balanced. Buy the team time.**

## My cues

**Calm. Talk. Carry. Connect. Delay.**

**These are principles, not restrictions. If you recognize a better solution, take it.**
