# Center Back (#4 / #5) — Position Card

## Every position, every game

- Scan before you receive, and look again after your first touch.
- Create an angle and become an outlet.
- Move on the pass. Move again after you pass.
- Small space: combine. Big space: exploit — carry open grass until a defender has to react.
- See the ball and the most dangerous space or opponent.
- On loss: counterpress if realistic; otherwise recover into shape.
- On regain: scan forward. Attack the advantage if it is there; otherwise secure possession.
- Use deception. Make brave, intelligent decisions.
- Know your restart jobs before kickoff.

**These are principles, not restrictions. If you recognize a better solution, take it.**

## My purpose

Protect central space, control depth, and begin attacks with composure.

## When we have it

- Scan and open your body before the pass arrives.
- Carry forward if nobody engages you, or carry sideways to change the angle.
- Find midfield feet when the lane is real.
- Switch when the strong side is crowded; keep the other center back and goalkeeper available.
- Use longer passes when they exploit real space.
- After passing into midfield, step up to support.

## When we lose it

- Protect the central route to goal first.
- Decide whether to step, delay, or recover.
- Communicate with the other center back, outside backs, and pivots.
- Buy time if the team is exposed.
- If an outside back is caught high, delay the counter until she recovers.

## When they have it

- See ball, runners, and dangerous space; manage the space behind the team.
- Ball pressured: hold the line or step up together.
- Ball unpressured: drop a few steps together. Moving as one line also manages offside.
- Step when covered; delay when exposed.
- Read the pass early and intercept when you can win it cleanly.
- Crosses: stay goal-side, see ball and runner, attack the ball in front of you.

**Do not run to where the attacker is. Run to where she is going.**

## When we win it

- Scan forward immediately.
- If a real transition advantage exists, use it.
- If not, secure the ball and start possession calmly.

## Players I connect with

- the other center back (#4 / #5)
- goalkeeper (#1)
- pivots (#6 / #8)
- outside backs (#2 / #3)

## Common traps

- automatic clearances or forced vertical passes
- dropping too early
- diving into counters
- watching the ball and losing runners
- standing square and closed when receiving

## My cues

**Open. Scan. Carry. Connect. Delay.**
