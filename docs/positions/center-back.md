# Center Back — Position Card

## Every position, every game

- Scan early.
- Create an angle and become an outlet.
- Move after the ball moves.
- On loss: counterpress if realistic, otherwise recover into shape.
- On regain: scan forward first, then secure possession if no advantage exists.
- Use deception when it helps.
- Make brave, intelligent decisions.

**These are principles, not restrictions. If you recognize a better solution, take it.**

## Primary purpose

Protect central space, control depth, and begin attacks with composure.

## When we have it

- Open body shape before receiving.
- Scan before the pass arrives.
- Carry forward if nobody engages you.
- Find midfield feet when the lane is genuinely available.
- Keep the other CB and goalkeeper available for circulation.
- Switch the point of attack when the strong side is crowded.
- Use longer passes intentionally when they exploit real space.

## When we lose it

- Protect the central route to goal first.
- Decide whether to step, delay, or recover.
- Communicate with the other CB, fullbacks, and pivot.
- Buy time if the team is exposed.

## When they have it

- See ball, runners, and dangerous space.
- Keep the line connected.
- Step when covered; delay when exposed.
- Use pursuit angles rather than simply chasing from behind.
- Manage space behind the team.

## When we win it

- Scan forward immediately.
- If a real transition advantage exists, use it.
- If not, secure the ball and start possession calmly.

## Players I connect with most

- other CB;
- goalkeeper;
- deepest midfielders;
- outside backs.

## Common traps

- automatic clearances;
- dropping too early;
- forcing vertical passes;
- diving into counters;
- watching the ball and losing runners;
- standing square and closed when receiving.

## Cues

**Open. Scan. Carry. Connect. Delay.**
