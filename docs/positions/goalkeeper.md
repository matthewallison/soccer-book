# Goalkeeper (#1) — Position Card

## My purpose

Protect the goal, organize the team in front of you, and be our eleventh possession player.

## Pregame mindset

**Calm and decisive.**

**If you decide to come, come. If you decide to play, play.** You don't need to save everything or pass perfectly — make the next decision completely. Talk early, before danger develops; your voice leads the team. After a mistake or a goal, get set and organize right away: **RESPOND. REFLECT. LEARN. NEXT PLAY.**

## Picture these moments

- Picture a back pass rolling to you: you open your body, touch it across, and play to the opposite side.
- Picture a center back under pressure: you move to an angle behind her and call early for the pass back.
- Picture catching the ball, scanning right away, and throwing or rolling it to an open teammate before the other team resets.
- Picture a through ball behind the back line: you decide to come, and come all the way to sweep it.
- Picture a cross coming in: you decide early, call it loudly, and claim it — or punch it clear.
- Picture getting set for a shot, then getting back up and organizing the team, whatever happened.

## When we have it

- Back passes are normal soccer, not emergencies. Use both feet.
- Read the press before the goal kick: short if they leave space, beyond them if they commit everyone.
- Distribute with intention. Punt or go long when it is truly better, not by habit.

## When we lose it

- Reorganize quickly behind the ball and communicate depth, runners, and danger.
- When our line is high, start higher too, so you can sweep behind it.

## When they have it

- Manage your depth relative to the back line.
- Point out runners your back line cannot see.

## When we win it

- Scan right away: can we restart possession quickly and intelligently?
- Ball in your hands? Release it within 8 seconds, or they get a corner kick.

**Quick possession before hopeful distance.**

## Important relationships

- Center backs (#4 / #5): be their outlet and extra eyes — tell them early to step, drop, or play back to you.
- Outside backs (#2 / #3) and pivots (#6 / #8): a quick throw or pass to a free one can beat the press.

## When you're unsure

**Get set. Communicate. Make the next decision fully.**

## My cues

**Organize. Decide. Commit. Distribute.**

**These are principles, not restrictions. If you recognize a better solution, take it.**
