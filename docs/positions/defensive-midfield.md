# Defensive Midfielder / Pivot (#6 / #8) — Position Card

## My purpose

Connect the team, protect the space underneath the ball, and help control the tempo of the game.

## Pregame mindset

**Want the ball.**

Don't hide when an opponent is close. Scan, find a better angle, and trust yourself to solve the pressure. Not every touch must speed us up: go forward, switch, carry, or keep the ball while the team reorganizes. **CALM MIND. FAST SOCCER.**

## Picture these moments

- Picture a center back passing to you: you check both shoulders, open your body, and know your next pass.
- Picture the ball moving away from you: you slide to a new angle so the next passer can find you.
- Picture a defender closing from behind: your first touch escapes her, and you switch the ball to the open side.
- Picture your partner dropping deeper to help the center backs: you rise higher.
- Picture our outside backs pushed high: you stay underneath the attack, ready to win the ball the moment we lose it.
- Picture open grass in front of you and nobody stepping: you carry until a defender has to react.

## When we have it

- Scan before receiving when there is time; receive side-on and move again after passing.
- Play forward when the advantage is real; recycle or switch when it is not.

## When we lose it

- Counterpress right away if the ball is close and someone protects underneath.
- Otherwise recover in front of the center backs.
- If an outside back is caught high, delay the counter until she recovers.

## When they have it

- Screen central passing lanes; step when the picture is covered, delay when exposed.
- Long ball from them: move to where it will land to win the second ball.
- Ball wide in our third: protect the top of the box and the cutback lane.

## When we win it

- Scan forward first: if an attacker is released into space, play quickly.
- If not, secure the first pass and help us organize.

## Important relationships

**One shows. One protects. One threatens.**

- You, the other pivot (#6 / #8), and the 10 are a rotating triangle, not three fixed dots. Who does each job can change.
- If one drops, another rises. If one advances, another protects underneath. If the 10 moves, the triangle adjusts.
- Your partner: stay at different heights, keep talking, and never both leave the center.
- Center backs (#4 / #5): give them an angle, protect the space in front of them, and delay counters together.

## When you're unsure

**Become an angle. Protect underneath.**

## My cues

**Scan. Angle. Connect. Protect.**

**These are principles, not restrictions. If you recognize a better solution, take it.**
