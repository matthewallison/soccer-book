# Position Cards

One-page pregame preparation and visualization cards. About 48 hours before a match, review the card for your expected role: its purpose, mindset, moments to picture, relationships, and what to do when you're unsure.

Numbers follow the common U.S. 1–11 numbering; the right side takes the lower number.

| Number | Card |
|---|---|
| #1 | [Goalkeeper](goalkeeper.md) |
| #2 / #3 | [Outside Back](outside-back.md) |
| #4 / #5 | [Center Back](center-back.md) |
| #6 / #8 | [Defensive Midfielder / Pivot](defensive-midfield.md) |
| #7 / #11 | [Winger](winger.md) |
| #9 | [Striker](striker.md) |
| #10 | [Attacking Midfielder](attacking-midfield.md) |

**These are principles, not restrictions. If you recognize a better solution, take it.**
