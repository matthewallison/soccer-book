# Striker (#9) — Position Card

## Every position, every game

- Scan before you receive, and look again after your first touch.
- Create an angle and become an outlet.
- Move on the pass. Move again after you pass.
- Small space: combine. Big space: exploit — carry open grass until a defender has to react.
- See the ball and the most dangerous space or opponent.
- On loss: counterpress if realistic; otherwise recover into shape.
- On regain: scan forward. Attack the advantage if it is there; otherwise secure possession.
- Use deception. Make brave, intelligent decisions.
- Know your restart jobs before kickoff.

**These are principles, not restrictions. If you recognize a better solution, take it.**

## My purpose

Occupy the center backs, create depth, connect attacks, and finish chances.

## When we have it

- Threaten the space behind so defenders cannot step up freely.
- Check toward the ball when it helps create a combination or opens space behind.
- Pin or move the center backs to create room for wingers and midfielders.
- Set passes for third-player combinations.
- Attack crosses, cutbacks, and second balls with purpose.
- Rotate with the wingers when it creates a better matchup or transition lane.

## When we lose it

- React immediately.
- Press if we lost it close to you and the team can support the press.
- Otherwise, block passes into the middle and steer play toward our pressing plan.

## When they have it

- Help make their buildup predictable instead of chasing every defender.
- Curve your pressing runs to block useful passing lanes.
- Stay connected enough that the team can press together.

## When we win it

- Decide quickly: run behind, show to feet, or drag a defender away for a teammate.
- If a faster winger has the better central lane, support her break.
- You do not have to be the highest runner every time.

## Players I connect with

- wingers (#7 / #11)
- the attacking midfielder (#10)
- pivots (#6 / #8) — especially the one who joins the attack

## Common traps

- making only straight runs
- always checking to the ball
- standing between the center backs without changing their decisions
- watching after laying the ball off
- forcing shots when a better final action exists

## My cues

**Threaten. Occupy. Connect. Move. Finish.**

## Film question

Did my movement change what the center backs had to decide?
