# Striker / 9 — Position Card

## Every position, every game

- Scan early.
- Create an angle and become an outlet.
- Move after the ball moves.
- On loss: counterpress if realistic, otherwise recover into shape.
- On regain: scan forward first, then secure possession if no advantage exists.
- Use deception when it helps.
- Make brave, intelligent decisions.

**These are principles, not restrictions. If you recognize a better solution, take it.**

## Primary purpose

Occupy center backs, create depth, connect attacks, and finish chances.

## When we have it

- Threaten behind so defenders cannot step freely.
- Check toward the ball when it helps create a combination or opens space behind.
- Pin or move center backs to create room for wingers and midfielders.
- Set passes for third-player combinations.
- Attack crosses, cutbacks, and second balls with purpose.
- Rotate with wide attackers when the movement creates a better matchup or transition lane.

## When we lose it

- React immediately.
- Press if the turnover is close and the team can support it.
- Otherwise help screen central buildup and guide play toward the team's pressing plan.

## When they have it

- Help make buildup predictable rather than chasing every defender.
- Curve pressing runs to block useful passing lanes.
- Stay connected enough that the team can press together.

## When we win it

- Decide quickly whether to run behind, show to feet, or move a defender for someone else's break.
- If a faster wide attacker has the best central transition lane, support the break rather than forcing yourself to remain the highest runner.

## Players I connect with most

- both wingers;
- attacking midfielder;
- nearest advanced midfielder.

## Common traps

- making only straight runs;
- always checking to the ball;
- standing between center backs without changing their decisions;
- watching after laying the ball off;
- forcing shots when a better final action exists.

## Cues

**Threaten. Occupy. Connect. Move them. Finish.**
