# Goalkeeper: Number One

You're playing goalkeeper today. The number one.

If you're somewhere you can, close your eyes.

Your purpose: protect the goal, organize the team in front of you, and be our eleventh possession player.

Your mindset: calm and decisive.

If you decide to come, come. If you decide to play, play. You don't need to save everything or pass perfectly. Make the next decision completely. Talk early, before danger develops; your voice leads the team. And after a mistake or a goal, get set and organize right away.

Say the next-play mantra with me. [pause] [slows down] Respond. Reflect. Learn. Next play.

Now take a breath. [pause] [slows down] Picture these moments.

A back pass is rolling to you. You open your body, touch it across, and play to the opposite side. [long pause]

A center back is under pressure. You move to an angle behind her, and call early for the pass back. [long pause]

You catch the ball. You scan right away, and throw or roll it to an open teammate before the other team resets. [long pause]

A through ball goes in behind the back line. You decide to come, and you come all the way to sweep it. [long pause]

A cross is coming in. You decide early, call it loudly, and claim it, or punch it clear. [long pause]

You get set for a shot. Then, whatever happened, you get back up and organize the team. [long pause]

Now, the four moments.

When we have it: back passes are normal soccer, not emergencies. Use both feet. Read the press before the goal kick. Go short if they leave space, and go beyond them if they commit everyone. Distribute with intention. Punt or go long when it's truly better, not out of habit.

When we lose it: reorganize quickly behind the ball, and communicate depth, runners, and danger. When our line is high, start higher too, so you can sweep behind it.

When they have it: manage your depth compared to the back line. Point out the runners your back line can't see.

When we win it: scan right away. Can we restart possession quickly and intelligently? If the ball is in your hands, release it within eight seconds, or they get a corner kick. Quick possession before hopeful distance.

Your important relationships.

Your center backs, the four and the five: be their outlet and their extra eyes. Tell them early to step, drop, or play back to you.

Your outside backs and pivots: a quick throw or pass to a free one can beat the press.

When you're unsure, what do you do? See if you can say it before I do. [long pause] Get set. Communicate. Make the next decision fully.

Your cues. Say them with me. [pause] [slows down] Organize. Decide. Commit. Distribute.

After the game, ask yourself: After I collected the ball, did I scan and restart possession quickly, or punt out of habit? Take that question with you.

These are principles, not restrictions. If you recognize a better solution, take it.
