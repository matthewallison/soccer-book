# Soccer Development Handbook: The Player Audio Edition

Welcome. This is the audio edition of the Soccer Development Handbook, and it's made for you, the player.

The handbook was written for an under-thirteen and under-fourteen girls' team. But the ideas aren't really about an age group. They work for players up through about sixteen, and honestly, well beyond.

Here's what it's all aiming at. The goal is not to turn you into a player who memorizes instructions and waits to be told what to do. The goal is a player who can scan, communicate, make intentional decisions, solve problems, compete, reflect, lead, and adapt. A player who can figure the game out for herself. And a player who understands how she learns, well enough to take part in her own development, instead of just being shaped by whatever coaching happens to reach her.

So this isn't meant to be background noise. Now and then, I'll ask you to say something with me: mostly our team mantras, and a few cues on the position cards. Say them out loud if you can, or in your head if you can't. And sometimes I'll ask you a question, and then stop talking for a moment. Use that pause. Actually think of an answer before I keep going.

Let's try one now. [pause] What's one thing you want to get better at this season? Not a vague thing. Something specific. [long pause] Hold on to that. We'll come back to it at the very end.

Everything in this handbook starts with four team mantras. We say them out loud, together. The practice mantra is the first thing we say at every practice, and the game mantra is the first thing we say in every game warm-up.

Say the practice mantra with me. [pause] [slows down] Scan. Talk. Intention. Faster.

Say the game mantra with me. [pause] [slows down] Calm mind. Fast soccer.

After a mistake, we use the next-play mantra. Say it with me. [pause] [slows down] Respond. Reflect. Learn. Next play.

And in pressure moments, we use the pressure mantra. Say it with me. [pause] [slows down] Want the moment.

Know those four by heart. You'll hear them again and again, because they carry everything else. When the game gets loud and fast, short words you already know are the ones that come back to you.

Here's how the rest of the audiobook is organized. First comes the High-Level Player Sheet, the short version of how we play and who we want to be. Then the chapters behind it: team culture, courage, and leadership; injuries and recovery; communication and movement; a chapter on how we play, and how coaching and training shape the way you learn it; film review; and the home development menu.

After that come seven position cards. Those are different. Each one is a short track, just a few minutes long, made to play before a match. Find your role, press play on the way to the game, and picture the moments before they happen.

Some things just work better on paper: printable sheets, links to sources, and the full coaching manual. You'll find all of those in the written handbook and on the website.
