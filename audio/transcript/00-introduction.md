# Soccer Development Handbook: The Player Audio Edition

Welcome. This is the audio edition of the Soccer Development Handbook, made for players.

The handbook was written for an under-thirteen and under-fourteen girls' team, and it's built around intelligent, technical, courageous soccer. The same ideas work for players up through about sixteen.

The central goal is not to create players who memorize instructions. It is to develop players who can scan, communicate, make intentional decisions, solve problems, compete, reflect, lead, and adapt.

Before we go any further, here's how this works. [pause] This isn't just something to listen to. Sometimes I'll ask you to say something with me. Say it out loud if you can, or in your head if you can't. And sometimes I'll ask you a question, and then pause. Use that pause. Actually think of an answer before I keep going.

Let's try one now. [pause] What's one thing you want to get better at this season? [long pause] Hold on to that. We'll come back to it at the very end.

Everything starts with four team mantras. We say them out loud, together. The practice mantra is the first thing we say at every practice, and the game mantra is the first thing we say in every game warm-up.

Say the practice mantra with me. [pause] [slows down] Scan. Talk. Intention. Faster.

Say the game mantra with me. [pause] [slows down] Calm mind. Fast soccer.

After a mistake, we use the next-play mantra. Say it with me. [pause] [slows down] Respond. Reflect. Learn. Next play.

And in pressure moments, we use the pressure mantra. Say it with me. [pause] [slows down] Want the moment.

Know those four by memory. You'll say them again and again in this audiobook, because they carry everything else.

Here's how this audio edition is organized. First comes the High-Level Player Sheet, the short version of how we play and who we want to be. Then come the chapters behind it: team culture, courage, and leadership; injuries and recovery; communication and movement; a chapter of ideas from the coaching manual that are worth hearing directly; film review; and the home development menu.

After that come seven position cards. Each one is a short track, just a few minutes long, made to play before a match. Find your role, listen, and picture the moments.

Some things work better on paper: printable sheets, links to sources, and the full coaching manual. You'll find those in the written handbook and on the website.
