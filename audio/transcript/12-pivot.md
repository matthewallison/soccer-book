# Pivot: Numbers Six and Eight

You're playing pivot today, one of our two deeper midfielders. The six or the eight.

If you're somewhere you can, close your eyes.

Your purpose: connect the team, protect the space underneath the ball, and help control the tempo of the game.

Your mindset: want the ball.

Don't hide when an opponent is close. Scan, find a better angle, and trust yourself to solve the pressure. Not every touch has to speed us up. Go forward, switch, carry, or keep the ball while the team reorganizes.

Say the game mantra with me. [pause] [slows down] Calm mind. Fast soccer.

Now take a breath. [pause] [slows down] Picture these moments.

A center back passes to you. You check both shoulders, open your body, and already know your next pass. [long pause]

The ball moves away from you. You slide to a new angle, so the next passer can find you. [long pause]

A defender closes from behind. Your first touch escapes her, and you switch the ball to the open side. [long pause]

Your partner drops deeper to help the center backs. You rise higher. [long pause]

Our outside backs are pushed high. You stay underneath the attack, ready to win the ball the moment we lose it. [long pause]

There's open grass in front of you, and nobody steps. You carry until a defender has to react. [long pause]

Now, the four moments.

When we have it: scan before receiving when there's time, receive side-on, and move again after passing. Play forward when the advantage is real. Recycle or switch when it isn't.

When we lose it: counterpress right away if the ball is close and someone is protecting underneath. Otherwise, recover in front of the center backs. If an outside back is caught high, delay the counter until she recovers.

When they have it: screen the central passing lanes. Step when you're covered; delay when you're exposed. On their long balls, move to where the ball will land, to win the second ball. When the ball is wide in our third, protect the top of the box and the cutback lane.

When we win it: scan forward first. If an attacker is released into space, play quickly. If not, secure the first pass and help us organize.

Your important relationships.

One shows. One protects. One threatens.

You, the other pivot, and the ten are a rotating triangle, not three fixed dots. Who does each job can change. If one drops, another rises. If one advances, another protects underneath. If the ten moves, the triangle adjusts.

Your partner: stay at different heights, keep talking, and never both leave the center.

Your center backs: give them an angle, protect the space in front of them, and delay counters together.

When you're unsure, what do you do? See if you can say it before I do. [long pause] Become an angle. Protect underneath.

Your cues. Say them with me. [pause] [slows down] Scan. Angle. Connect. Protect.

After the game, ask yourself: Before I received, did I know where the pressure was, and was I at a different height from my partner? Take that question with you.

These are principles, not restrictions. If you recognize a better solution, take it.
