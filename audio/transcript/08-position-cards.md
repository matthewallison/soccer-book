# The Position Cards

The next seven tracks are the position cards. Each one is a short pregame preparation and visualization for one role.

About two days before a match, listen to the card for your expected role. You can listen again on the way to the game. If you're somewhere you can, close your eyes. When a track asks you to picture a moment, really see it: the field, your teammates, the ball arriving. I'll pause after each moment to give you time.

Each card covers your purpose, your pregame mindset, moments to picture, the four moments of the game, your most important relationships, what to do when you're unsure, and a few cues to carry onto the field. Near the end, I'll ask what to do when you're unsure. Try to answer before I do; it gets easier every time you listen. Then say your cues with me, out loud if you can.

Don't try to memorize a checklist. Picture the moments.

The numbers follow the common U.S. numbering, one through eleven, and the right side of the field takes the lower number. The cards, in order, are: goalkeeper, the number one. Outside back, the two and the three. Center back, the four and the five. Pivot, the six and the eight. Winger, the seven and the eleven. Striker, the nine. And attacking midfielder, the ten.

These are principles, not restrictions. If you recognize a better solution, take it.
