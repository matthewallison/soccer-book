# Attacking Midfielder: Number Ten

You're playing attacking midfielder today. The ten.

If you're somewhere you can, close your eyes.

Your purpose: connect midfield to attack, find useful space between the lines, and help create chances.

Your mindset: want the ball in difficult spaces.

You are allowed to try the turn, the final pass, the dribble, or the combination. Some won't work. Don't let one failed idea make you cautious.

Say the next-play mantra with me. [pause] [slows down] Respond. Reflect. Learn. Next play.

Now take a breath. [pause] [slows down] Picture these moments.

You scan, check into a pocket between the lines, and receive on the half-turn. [long pause]

The nine checks short. You run beyond her, and become the highest attacker, for now. [long pause]

The nine pins both center backs, while you show underneath her and turn. [long pause]

You drift into the half-space, between wide and center, and play a wall pass with a winger. [long pause]

Nobody steps to you. You carry at the defense, and sell a fake until someone has to choose. [long pause]

You help out deeper, then arrive at the top of the box just in time for the cutback. [long pause]

Now, the four moments.

When we have it: One shows. One protects. One threatens. You and the pivots are a rotating triangle, and who does each job can change. Don't park. Show between the lines, drop to connect, find a half-space, or run behind. Small space: combine. Big space: exploit. Carry, switch, or release a runner.

When we lose it: react immediately. You're often the closest, so counterpress if support is near. Otherwise, recover into midfield and protect central space.

When they have it: know the plan. Either step up beside the nine, or screen their deepest midfielder. Stay connected to the pivots, and screen passes into the middle. Recognize pressing triggers, instead of chasing every pass.

When we win it: look forward. Release a runner, or attack the middle before they recover. If not, help keep the ball while we reorganize.

Your important relationships.

One comes. One goes.

The striker, the nine: if she checks, you can run beyond her. If you check, she stays high and pins the center backs. You and the nine can keep cycling during an attack. Positions set the shape; movement changes who fills each space.

The pivots, the six and the eight: if you drop, one of them can rise. If one advances, another protects underneath.

The wingers: combine in the half-space, and rotate without losing our width.

When you're unsure, what do you do? See if you can say it before I do. [long pause] Find a different height than the nine, and become available.

Your cues. Say them with me. [pause] [slows down] Scan. Find space. Connect. Arrive.

After the game, ask yourself: Did I find space between the lines, and did I move again after I passed? Take that question with you.

These are principles, not restrictions. If you recognize a better solution, take it.
