# Center Back: Numbers Four and Five

You're playing center back today. The four or the five.

If you're somewhere you can, close your eyes.

Your purpose: protect the center, lead the back line, and start our attacks with composure.

Your mindset: calm is a weapon.

Safe does not automatically mean clearance. Near your own goal, if you have time and space, solve the soccer problem, even after a pass goes wrong. Talk early: tell the line to step, drop, or cover.

Say the game mantra with me. [pause] [slows down] Calm mind. Fast soccer.

Now take a breath. [pause] [slows down] Picture these moments.

You receive from the goalkeeper. You check your shoulder, open your body, and touch it forward. [long pause]

Nobody is pressing you. You carry until a defender steps, then find the midfielder she left. [long pause]

The side with the ball is getting crowded. You switch it to the open side. [long pause]

Their press is pushing high, and the far-side winger is free. You hit a purposeful diagonal into her path. [long pause]

An attacker is running at you on a counter. You stay on your feet, slow her down, and wait for help. [long pause]

Your partner steps to win the ball. You tuck in behind her and cover the space she left. [long pause]

Now, the four moments.

When we have it: your partner is an option, not an automatic pass. Carry sideways to change the angle when the forward lane is closed. Find a midfielder's feet when the lane is real, and after you pass, step up to support.

When we lose it: protect the central route to goal first, then step, delay, or recover. If an outside back is caught high, delay the counter until she recovers. Recover on an angle toward goal, not by chasing.

When they have it: if the ball is pressured, hold the line or push up together. If the ball is unpressured, drop a few steps together; moving as one line manages offside. On crosses, stay goal-side, see the ball and the runner, and attack the ball in front of you. Read the pass early, and intercept when you can win it.

When we win it: scan forward. If there's a real transition advantage, use it. If not, secure the ball and start possession calmly.

Your important relationships.

One steps. One covers.

The other center back: don't both go to the same attacker, and don't both leave her free.

The pivots, the six and the eight: they give you an angle and protect the space in front of you. Delay counters together.

Your goalkeeper: she's your outlet, and your eyes behind you. Keep her available, and listen to her calls.

Your outside backs: use them as early wide outlets, and keep them connected to your line.

When you're unsure, what do you do? See if you can say it before I do. [long pause] Protect the center. Stay balanced. Buy the team time.

Your cues. Say them with me. [pause] [slows down] Calm. Talk. Carry. Connect. Delay.

After the game, ask yourself: When nobody pressured me, did I carry or find a midfielder's feet, or did I clear it? Take that question with you.

These are principles, not restrictions. If you recognize a better solution, take it.
